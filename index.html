Most hacks start with a message someone believed.

PhishCheck is a short course on recognising fake emails, texts and phone calls. It takes about twenty minutes and you don't need any technical background.

How the course runs

  • Types of phishing

    Email is only one door. Texts, phone calls, fake websites and impersonated bosses all work the same way.

  • Spot the red flags

    Open a fake email and a real one, and click the details that give each away.

  • Real cases

    Four incidents that happened to careful, well-resourced organisations.

  • Stay safe

    Habits that help, and a plan for the moment after you realise you clicked.

  • Glossary

    Plain-English meanings for the jargon you'll run into, from MFA to spoofing.

  • Checklist

    A one-page list to check before you click. Print it or keep it open.

  • Quiz

    Twenty questions in a random order. Some of the emails are genuine, because not everything is a scam.
